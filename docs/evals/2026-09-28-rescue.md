# 2026-09-28: task rescues, live on Emirates

This eval measures what [`specs/task-rescue.md`](../specs/task-rescue.md) does
on a real booking site. The setup matches the deliberation eval
([`2026-09-28-deliberation.md`](2026-09-28-deliberation.md)), so the runs are
directly comparable:

- the same saved 26-step Mumbai → Dubai plan, run through `task_live` with
  `FLOW_FILE`;
- the person's own Chrome, on `:9222`;
- deliberation `deep`;
- three rescues allowed per task, answered by `openai/gpt-6-luna` at low
  reasoning effort.

There were two runs. Each stopped before payment.

**Verdict:** both runs got past a step that every earlier run had stalled on.
The furthest point on the plan moved from step 18 to step 20 of 26. Neither
run reached the payment checkpoint.

| Run | Rescued at | Plan step reached | Ended on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earlier `deep`, 12 runs | none | 10 (×6), 13, 18 at best | calendar left open, fare press, gender |
| rescue 1 | 13 → recovered; 14; the rescue's own step | 14 | "Change search" pressed with no effect |
| rescue 2 | 11 → recovered; 13 → recovered; 18 | 20 | the rescue's own `verify`, asked too early |

## What the rescuer saw and did

- **The search that did not register** (run 2, plan step 11). The rescuer read
  from the screen that "the departure airport, arrival airport, and departure
  date are missing". The earlier `choose` steps had reported success without
  their choices taking. It re-entered all three and searched, and the search
  went through.
- **The fare card** (plan step 13, in both runs). This is the step that
  stalled the fare-press runs of the earlier eval.
  - Run 1 replaced it with "choose Economy in the cabin tabs".
  - Run 2 named the card outright: "Open the Economy Class fare options for
    the 04:25 Mumbai to Dubai flight priced at INR 21,988".
  - Both recovered, and run 2 continued to the passenger page.
- **The wrong date** (run 1). The rescuer noticed that the results were for
  Monday 19 October, not the requested Sunday 18th. The date `choose` had
  falsely reported success, a gap no Jev check had caught. It planned a new
  search, but its first step, "change the search" (then "open Change search"),
  changed nothing on screen, and the rescues ran out.
- **The passenger form** (run 2, plan step 18). The plan asked for a gender, a
  nationality, and a birth date. The form asks for none of these.
  - The rescuer replaced them with the contact fields the form does show, and
    they were filled.
  - It also added the plan's final `verify` too early, before the extras, and
    that check failed at 0.36, which ended the run.

## Findings

1. **The rescues were right about the cause.** Every reason the model gave
   matched the screen, the final screenshot, or the run's own reports. This
   includes the two false successes, the unregistered search and the wrong
   date, which neither Jev nor reflection had caught.
2. **Guidance can only replace the failed step.** In run 2, rescue 3 covered
   plan steps 18 to 20, but steps 19 and 20 stayed in the flow after it. The
   rescuer has no way to say "this also covers the next two steps". Letting it
   say so, while never skipping past a `stop_before`, is the obvious next
   change.
3. **Rescues can be spent on side paths.** Run 1 used its last two rescues on
   the date problem and never returned to the plan's fare step 14. With three
   rescues, a correct diagnosis still needs guidance that Jev can carry out.
4. **Cost.** Each rescue took seconds to about a minute. That is small next
   to the minutes of Jev calls in a run. Run 1 took 14 minutes and run 2 took
   7.5.

Two runs are anecdotes, not a rate. The live-site noise described in the
deliberation eval still applies.

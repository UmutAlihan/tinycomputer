# 2026-09-28: task rescues, live on Emirates

This eval measures what [`specs/task-rescue.md`](../specs/task-rescue.md) does
on a real booking site. The setup matches the deliberation eval
([`2026-09-28-deliberation.md`](2026-09-28-deliberation.md)), so the runs are
directly comparable:

- the same saved 26-step Mumbai → Dubai plan, run through `task_live` with
  `FLOW_FILE`;
- the person's own Chrome, on `:9222`;
- deliberation `deep`;
- three rescues allowed per task, answered by `openai/gpt-6-luna` at low
  reasoning effort.

There were five runs. None paid.

**Verdict:** the first complete Emirates run. Run 5, with `covers` and field
values on, reached the payment checkpoint in 4.4 minutes and stopped there.
Before rescues, no run had got past step 18 of 26. (Run 4 is left out: the
laptop's lid closed 20 seconds in.)

| Run | Rescued at | Plan step reached | Ended on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earlier `deep`, 12 runs | none | 10 (×6), 13, 18 at best | calendar left open, fare press, gender |
| rescue 1 | 13 → recovered; 14; the rescue's own step | 14 | "Change search" pressed with no effect |
| rescue 2 | 11 → recovered; 13 → recovered; 18 | 20 | the rescue's own `verify`, asked too early |
| rescue 3, with `covers` | 1 → recovered; 18 with `covers: 2` → recovered; 25 | 25 | the plan's `verify` of the typed contact details |

## What the rescuer saw and did

- **The search that did not register** (run 2, plan step 11). The rescuer read
  from the screen that "the departure airport, arrival airport, and departure
  date are missing". The earlier `choose` steps had reported success without
  their choices taking. It re-entered all three and searched, and the search
  went through.
- **The fare card** (plan step 13, in both runs). This is the step that
  stalled the fare-press runs of the earlier eval.
  - Run 1 replaced it with "choose Economy in the cabin tabs".
  - Run 2 named the card outright: "Open the Economy Class fare options for
    the 04:25 Mumbai to Dubai flight priced at INR 21,988".
  - Both recovered, and run 2 continued to the passenger page.
- **The wrong date** (run 1). The rescuer noticed that the results were for
  Monday 19 October, not the requested Sunday 18th. The date `choose` had
  falsely reported success, a gap no Jev check had caught. It planned a new
  search, but its first step, "change the search" (then "open Change search"),
  changed nothing on screen, and the rescues ran out.
- **The passenger form** (run 2, plan step 18). The plan asked for a gender, a
  nationality, and a birth date. The form asks for none of these.
  - The rescuer replaced them with the contact fields the form does show, and
    they were filled.
  - It also added the plan's final `verify` too early, before the extras, and
    that check failed at 0.36, which ended the run.

## Run 3, with `covers`

This run used the same setup, after `covers` was added.

- **Step 1.** The page load timed out, but the page was there. The rescuer
  replaced the `browse` with a `wait_for` on the booking form, and the run
  went on.
- **Steps 13 and 14 passed without help** this time.
- **The passenger form** (step 18). The rescuer wrapped the gender and
  nationality in `if`s, since neither field was shown, and filled the email
  and mobile number. It declared `covers: 2`, which dropped plan steps 19 and
  20. The extras checks (21 to 24) then ran as planned.
- **The final `verify`** (step 25). "The passenger name, contact email, and
  mobile number are shown correctly" failed at 0.32. A rescue re-entered the
  fields and asked again, and the check failed at 0.38.
  - Task runs keep `include_values` off, so neither Jev nor the rescuer's
    screen text sees what was typed into a field. To both of them, the filled
    form looks empty.
  - A `verify` of typed values therefore could not pass. Tasks now run with
    `include_values` on, with secrets masked, and the rescuer's screen text
    shows what each field holds, redacted, so the check can pass.

## Run 5, with field values

This run used tasks with `include_values` on, secrets masked.

- **Steps 1 to 17 passed without help.**
- **Step 18.** A rescue replaced the gender and nationality steps with the
  contact fields and a `verify`, with `covers: 2`.
- **The contact `verify`** held at 0.92, and the plan's own step 25 held at
  0.91. Both had failed at 0.32 to 0.38 without field values.
- **Step 26, `stop_before` paying.** It found no payment control: the
  passenger page leads to "Continue to Options" first. The rescuer made steps
  to continue to Options, wait for it, decline the extras, and add a
  `stop_before` for the Payment step. That `stop_before` was gated in front
  of "Continue to Payment", and the task ended at the payment checkpoint.
- **A safety gap this exposed.** Nothing forced guidance that replaces a
  failed `stop_before` to keep a guard. It must now hold one, or it goes back
  as invalid.

## Kashmir: Delhi → Srinagar on IndiGo (23 steps)

This run used the saved plan, the same setup, and three rescues. It took 7.4
minutes.

- **IndiGo returned real results this time.** The run searched, picked the
  cheapest fare (₹7,346 Saver), and pressed "continue to passenger details".
- **Rescue 1** noticed that the page never left the results: "continue"
  reported success but did not advance. Its steps re-selected the fare and
  continued, which reached the passenger form. Its own combined "enter
  everything" step then stalled.
- **Rescue 2** used the form's own labels ("first and middle name", "contact
  email"), chose the gender, and filled the contacts, with `covers: 1`. All
  of it recovered. The seat, meal, and insurance checks then passed.
- **Rescue 3.** Plan step 21, "decline all other paid extras", stalled on
  the passenger form's cab, IndiGo Switch, and Fare Hold offers. These are
  unselected by default. The rescuer's first step, "Leave … unselected",
  asked for no action, so it changed nothing and failed. The rescues were
  then spent, at plan step 21 of 23: the same step as the best earlier run.

The protocol now says so: every step must change something on the screen,
and leaving an offer alone means writing no step for it and moving on.

## Findings

1. **The rescues were right about the cause.** Every reason the model gave
   matched the screen, the final screenshot, or the run's own reports. This
   includes the two false successes, the unregistered search and the wrong
   date, which neither Jev nor reflection had caught.
2. **Guidance could only replace the failed step.** In run 2, rescue 3 covered
   plan steps 18 to 20, but steps 19 and 20 stayed in the flow after it. The
   rescuer had no way to say "this also covers the next two steps". It can
   now: `covers` drops them, and it may never cover a `stop_before`. This has
   not been re-run live yet.
3. **Rescues can be spent on side paths.** Run 1 used its last two rescues on
   the date problem and never returned to the plan's fare step 14. With three
   rescues, a correct diagnosis still needs guidance that Jev can carry out.
4. **Cost.** Each rescue took seconds to about a minute. That is small next
   to the minutes of Jev calls in a run. Run 1 took 14 minutes and run 2 took
   7.5.

Five runs are anecdotes, not a rate. The live-site noise described in the
deliberation eval still applies.

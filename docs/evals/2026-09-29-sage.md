# 2026-09-29: Levanto Sage in place of Jev, live

This eval compares Levanto Sage (`levanto-sage-v1.2`) with Jev
(`typesafe/jev-1.13`) as the decision model behind the same flows, on the
same two live bookings. The adapter is `crates/tinycomputer-engine/src/agentic/sage/`.

The setup is otherwise identical to the passing runs in
[`2026-09-28-rescue.md`](2026-09-28-rescue.md):

- the committed plans, `tasks/emirates/plan.json` and `tasks/kashmir/plan.json`,
  replayed with `task_live`;
- the person's own Chrome on `:9222`;
- deliberation `deep`, with 7 votes;
- up to 5 rescues on `openai/gpt-6-luna`.

Sage ran in its default modes: reasoning `auto` and choices at `quality`.
There was one run per site, so these numbers are indicative, not averages.

## How the costs are counted

- **Jev.** The input tokens in the journal times OpenRouter's price. Jev's
  output is free.
- **Sage.** Levanto bills a unit per question or per 4,000 input tokens,
  whichever is higher, with reasoning free. The count here applies that rule
  to every journaled call.
- **Rescues** run on GPT 6 Luna either way and add under a cent each.

## Findings

- **Sage works as a drop-in.** Emirates reached the payment checkpoint
  through the adapter alone. The irreversible-press gate held: a rescue's
  "continue to the payment page" was refused, and the next rescue put a
  `stop_before` in its place.
- **Each call is 10 to 15 times slower.** A decision waits for its slowest of
  7 framings, so runs took 2.5 to 10 times longer. That cost the Kashmir
  booking: after 24 minutes, IndiGo's session expired on the seat page, one
  page before payment.
- **It costs about 13 times more per run here.** Every Jev request carries
  several questions, the page kind included, and each counts as a Sage unit.
  Every decision is also asked 7 ways.
- **No accuracy gain in these runs.** Sage needed twice the rescues. On
  Kashmir it stumbled on steps Jev passed cleanly: choosing Srinagar,
  opening the fare options, and continuing past the passenger form.

## What would make a fairer comparison

Sage calibrates its own probabilities and reasons for free, so the 7-vote
framing is largely redundant for it.

- **Units and cost.** Asking each decision once would cut units about
  7-fold: roughly $0.26 for an Emirates run on the Pro plan.
- **Latency.** Each decision would stop waiting on the slowest of 7 calls.

`SAGE_FAST=1` (choices in one pass) is the other lever not yet measured.
